GamePilot (Lite) · Effective November 12, 2026

The short version

GamePilot Lite does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The games you score — team names, colors, scores, clocks and settings — are saved only on your own device so you can pick them back up later. Deleting a game, or deleting the app, removes them.

Local network

If you choose to link devices (for example, using a second iPad as the big screen), GamePilot Lite uses your local network or Bluetooth to send the game's score and clock directly between your own nearby devices. Nothing is sent to us or to any server, and linking only happens with a device showing the 4-digit code you enter.

Children

GamePilot Lite collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Questions? Email jeff@crmb.io.
