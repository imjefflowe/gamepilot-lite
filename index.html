Common questions

How do I start a game?

Tap Start a game, pick your sport, set team names, colors and rules, then tap Start. Every control for that sport fits on one screen.

How do I put the scoreboard on a TV or projector?
  1. iPhone or iPad: open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring and choose your TV — or plug in an HDMI adapter. The TV shows only the scoreboard; your buttons stay on your device.
  2. Mac: open Control Center → Screen Mirroring, pick your TV and choose Extended display. In GamePilot Lite, click Show on display and pick the TV. Press Esc or ⌘. to stop.
How do I use a second device as the big screen?

On the device for the big screen, open GamePilot Lite and choose Be the big screen — it shows a 4-digit code. On the device keeping score, tap the TV button and enter that code. Both devices need to be on the same Wi-Fi or near each other with Bluetooth on, and GamePilot Lite needs Local Network permission (Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network).

How do I set the clock to a specific time?

Tap the clock (or Set time) and pick the minutes and seconds. In basketball you can set the shot clock there too.

Can I turn off the shot clock or use double bonus?

Yes. Both are in the basketball rules when you start a game, and in the ⋯ menu during a game.

I tapped the wrong button — how do I fix it?

Tap Undo at the top. You can also adjust any number with the − and + buttons. In volleyball, pickleball and tennis, Reopen last set puts a finished set back in play at its final score.

Where are my old games?

Every game is saved on your device. From the start screen, open Older games and tap one to pick it back up. Swipe left on a game to delete it.

Do I need an account or internet?

No. GamePilot Lite works offline, has no account and no ads, and collects no data. See the privacy policy.
